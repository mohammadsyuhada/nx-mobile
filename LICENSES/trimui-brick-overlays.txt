The console overlays in assets/overlays/ (FC, GB, GBA, GBC, GG, LYNX, MD, MGBA, NGPC, SFC, SUPA; 720p and 768p) are
Shin's (KrutzOtrem) Trimui Brick overlays, https://github.com/KrutzOtrem/Trimui-Brick-Overlays (commit f4ab2131),
brought in through nx-redux. The author's notes: artwork recreated by pixel-tracing high-resolution photographs of the
original hardware, then upscaled and retouched; some reference images adapted from each console's Wikipedia page.
Console names and designs are trademarks of their owners. The repository's licence follows.
